THEORY, MATHEMATICS & ALGORITHMS

Mathematical & Algorithmic Foundations

Comprehensive analysis of text preprocessing, feature vectorization, probabilistic formulation, and maximum-margin hyperplane optimization.

01

Text Cleaning & Token Normalization

Raw emails contain obfuscated HTML, deceptive hyperlinks, and variable formatting. The preprocessing pipeline applies uniform regex normalization:

  • Strips raw HTML tags and unescapes XML/HTML entities
  • Normalizes URLs into uniform httpaddr tokens
  • Normalizes emails into emailaddr tokens
  • Normalizes currency amounts into dollar_amount tokens
  • Masks digits into num_token, lowercases and removes punctuation
02

TF-IDF n-Gram Vectorization

Maps unstructured text into a 15,000-dimensional numerical space. Sublinear Term Frequency prevents repetitive spam words from dominating:

\text{TF-IDF}(t, d) = (1 + \log(\text{TF}(t, d))) \times \log\left(\frac{1 + N}{1 + \text{DF}(t)}\right) + 1

Extracts unigrams and key bigrams (e.g. "claim prize", "wire transfer") fit exclusively on the training partition to prevent leakage.

03

Linear Support Vector Machine (Best)

Finds the optimal separating hyperplane maximizing the geometric margin between classes:

\min_{\mathbf{w}, b, \xi} \frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{w}\|^2 + C \sum_{i=1}^{n} \xi_i \quad \text{s.t.} \quad y_i(\mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{x}_i + b) \ge 1 - \xi_i

Linear SVM outperformed all candidates with 99.29% accuracy and 99.71% precision on sparse high-dimensional text.

04

Rule-Based Phishing & URL Engine

Complements statistical inference with heuristic risk rules:

  • URL Threat Analysis: Flags IP hosts, shorteners, and abused TLDs (.xyz, .top, .click)
  • Brand Spoofing: Detects typosquatting domains (e.g. paypal-security-alert.xyz)
  • Phishing Patterns: Scans for credential harvesting, urgent account suspension, and wire transfers
  • Zero-Trust Notice: Never guarantees safety. Low-risk result mandates defense-in-depth
