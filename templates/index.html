THEORY & MATHEMATICS

How SpamGuard AI Works

A rigorous 4-stage machine learning workflow transforming unstructured raw text into mathematical probability distributions.

01

Text Cleaning & Token Normalization

Raw emails contain irregular formatting, HTML tags, and evasive obfuscation. Our pipeline applies regex normalization:

  • Strips raw HTML tags and unescapes entities
  • Normalizes URLs into uniform httpaddr tokens
  • Normalizes emails into emailaddr tokens
  • Masks monetary currencies (dollar_amount)
  • Lowercases text and strips stopwords
02

TF-IDF Feature Vectorization

Converts clean words into a 15,000-dimensional numerical space. Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency prevents frequent words from dominating:

\text{TF-IDF}(t, d) = \text{TF}(t, d) \times \log\left(\frac{1 + N}{1 + \text{DF}(t)}\right) + 1

Features incorporate unigrams and bigrams (e.g. "claim prize", "wire transfer") with sublinear logarithmic scaling.

03

Multinomial Naive Bayes

Computes class posteriors using Bayes' Theorem under the conditional independence assumption:

P(C_k \mid \mathbf{x}) \propto P(C_k) \prod_{i=1}^{n} P(x_i \mid C_k)^{x_i}

Laplace smoothing (α = 0.1) prevents zero-frequency zero-probability errors for rare unseen words.

04

Risk Level & Decision Boundary

Posterior probabilities are mapped into operational cybersecurity severity tiers:

  • Critical Risk (≥85%): Confirmed malware/phishing.
  • High Risk (50-84%): Unsolicited promotional spam.
  • Medium (25-49%): Borderline marketing emails.
  • Safe (<25%): Standard authenticated correspondence.
